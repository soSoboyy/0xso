#### Security Management Maintenance Models:

		- Provides managerial guidance for establishing and implementing an information security program.
		- Addresses the ongoing tasks expected from an Information Security Manager.

##### Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC):

- SDLC is the overall process of developing, implementing, and retiring information systems through a multistep approach—initiation, analysis, design, implementation, and maintenance.
- Each phase of the SDLC includes a minimum set of information security activities required to incorporate security into a system effectively

	6. Performance measures:
		1. Information security metrics should be used for monitoring the performance of information security controls and initiating performance improvements.
		2. ==Important: 60% rule for exploring issues of systems and network performance.
			1. Example -->  Memory Usage:  When the memory usage associated with a particular CPU‐based system averages or exceeds 60 % over prolonged periods, consider adding more memory.
		
	7.  Security Planning:  Strategic, tactical, and operating plans must be developed that align with and support organisational and IT plans, goals, and objectives.
	
	8. IT Contingency Planning: Contingency planning consists of a process for recovery and documentation of procedures for conducting recovery.
		 Periodic reviews of the plan must be conducted to ensure the current information about: 
			1.  Key personnel and vendor information
			2. System components and dependencies
			3. Recovery strategy, vital records, and operating requirements.
			
	9. Risk Management : - The principal goal of risk management is to protect the organization and its ability to perform its mission, not just protect its information assets.
		==1. Risk cannot be eliminated entirely.
		2. Many risk management activities are conducted during a snapshot in time. (A static representation of a dynamic environment.)
		
	10. Certification, Accreditation, and Security Assessments
		 Some organisations need to review their own systems for certification and accreditation to be in compliance with banking, healthcare, international, or other regulations.

	12. Incident Response: Preventing IS from attacks would be ideal, but not all security incidents can be prevented.
		1. Org. that depends on IS and Networks should identify and assess the risks to its systems by reducing them to an acceptable level.
		
	13. Configuration and Change Management (CCM)
		1. CCM is to manage the effects of changes or differences in configurations in an information system or network.
		2. By the CCM process, system changes must be tested before implementation to observe the effects of the change and minimise the risk of adverse results.
